# Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** January 15, 2025

## Overview

At KRITIKA Beauty SALON, we understand that plans can change. This policy outlines our cancellation and refund procedures to ensure fairness for both our clients and our business operations.

## Cancellation Time Frames

### Free Cancellation
**More than 24 hours before appointment:**
- ✅ Full refund or reschedule at no charge
- ✅ No cancellation fee
- ✅ Loyalty points retained
- ✅ Process time: 5-7 business days

### Standard Cancellation
**6-24 hours before appointment:**
- ⚠️ 50% cancellation fee applies
- ✅ 50% refund to original payment method
- ✅ Or full credit for future services
- ⚠️ Loyalty points may be partially deducted
- ⏱️ Process time: 5-7 business days

### Late Cancellation
**Less than 6 hours before appointment:**
- ❌ 100% cancellation fee
- ❌ No refund
- ❌ May result in loyalty points deduction
- ⚠️ May affect future booking privileges

### No-Show
**Failure to arrive without notice:**
- ❌ 100% charge (full service amount)
- ❌ No refund
- ❌ 50-100 loyalty points deducted
- ⚠️ May restrict future bookings for 30 days
- ⚠️ Multiple no-shows may result in account suspension

## Refund Process

### Refund Methods
Refunds will be issued to:
- **Original Payment Method** (preferred)
- **Store Credit** (instant)
- **Bank Transfer** (for cash payments)

### Processing Time
- **Credit/Debit Card:** 5-7 business days
- **UPI/Digital Wallets:** 3-5 business days
- **Store Credit:** Instant
- **Bank Transfer:** 7-10 business days

### Refund Calculation
```
Refund Amount = Service Cost - Cancellation Fee - Processing Fee (if applicable)
```

**Example:**
- Service Cost: ₹2,000
- Cancelled 12 hours before: 50% fee = ₹1,000
- Refund: ₹1,000

### Store Credit Option
- **Bonus:** Get 110% value as store credit
- **Validity:** 6 months from issue date
- **Usage:** Any service, any time
- **Transferable:** No
- **Expires:** Cannot be extended

## FAQs

**Q: Can I cancel my appointment after check-in?**
A: No, once you've checked in, the service is considered started. No refunds apply.

**Q: What if I'm running late?**
A: Please inform us immediately. We'll hold your slot for 15 minutes. Beyond that, it may be treated as late cancellation.

**Q: Can someone else use my booking?**
A: Bookings are non-transferable without prior approval. Contact us to modify booking details.

**Q: What if the service was unsatisfactory?**
A: This is not covered under cancellation policy. Please refer to our Quality Guarantee Policy.

**Q: Do weather conditions qualify for free cancellation?**
A: Extreme weather with government advisories qualifies. Regular rain doesn't automatically qualify.
