## Party Makeup Basics {#party-makeup-basics}

### Why Party Makeup is Different:
- **Long-lasting** (4-6 hours through dancing and eating)
- **Camera-friendly** for photos and videos
- **Bold and noticeable** in dim lighting
- **Coordinates with outfit** color and style

### Universal Base Preparation
**What You Need:**
- Primer (oil-free for oily skin)
- Foundation (your exact shade)
- Concealer (1-2 shades lighter)
- Compact powder
- Beauty blender or makeup sponge

**Process:**
1. Cleanse and pat dry face
2. Apply moisturizer, wait 5 minutes
3. Focus primer on T-zone, forehead, nose, chin
4. Apply foundation in dots and blend outward/upward
5. Conceal dark circles with triangle technique
6. Set gently with loose powder

## Golden Rules of Party Makeup {#golden-rules}

| Rule | Do | Don't |
|------|----|-------|
| **Feature Focus** | Heavy Eyes + Nude Lips OR Simple Eyes + Bold Lips | Heavy Eyes + Bold Lips |
| **Outfit First** | Plan makeup after choosing dress | Ignore outfit color/style |
| **Lighting Test** | Check in natural daylight and artificial light | Rely only on bathroom lighting |
| **Skin Prep** | Moisturize, prime, use setting spray | Skip primer and skin preparation |

## Traditional Makeup for Saree {#saree-makeup}

### Key Elements
- **Base:** Full coverage, matte finish
- **Eyes:** Dramatic, defined (Golds, Coppers, Browns)
- **Lips:** Bold or Nude (Red, Maroon, Nude)
- **Cheeks:** Soft glow (Rosy pink, Peach)
- **Accessories:** Bindi essential

### Eye Makeup Process
1. **Transition Shade:** Light brown in crease
2. **Main Color:** Gold or Copper on entire lid
3. **Depth:** Dark brown in outer V-shape
4. **Highlight:** Champagne in inner corner
5. **Eyeliner:** Medium thick wing with kajal in waterline

### Saree Color to Lip Color Guide

| Saree Color | Best Lip Colors |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Red | Deep red, Maroon, Nude |
| Blue | Pink, Coral, Nude |
| Green | Nude, Peach, Soft pink |
| Black | Bold red, Wine, Nude |

**Best For:** Weddings, Pujas, Traditional functions  
**Time Required:** 60-75 minutes  
**Professional Cost:** ₹3,000 - ₹6,000 in Patna

## Glamorous Look for Lehenga {#lehenga-makeup}

### Key Elements
- **Base:** Full coverage HD
- **Eyes:** Smokey, dramatic with shimmer
- **Lips:** Nude or Bold (depending on lehenga color)
- **Contouring:** Sculpted, very heavy
- **Lashes:** False, dramatic

### Lehenga Color Lip Coordination

| Lehenga Color | Lip Options |
|---------------|-------------|
| Red | Nude, Matching red, Deep maroon |
| Pink/Fuchsia | Light pink, Mauve, Nude |
| Blue | Pink, Nude |
| Green | Nude, Coral |

**Best For:** Weddings, Sangeet, Reception, Grand celebrations  
**Time Required:** 90-120 minutes  
**Professional Cost:** ₹5,000 - ₹10,000 in Patna

## Common Party Makeup Mistakes {#mistakes}

| Mistake | Problem | Solution |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Wrong Foundation Shade | Face and neck different colors | Test on jawline in natural light |
| Heavy Eyes + Heavy Lips | Over-the-top, costume-y look | Follow golden rule: one feature focus |
| Ignoring the Outfit | Makeup and dress clash | Plan makeup after outfit selection |
| Skipping Primer | Makeup becomes patchy quickly | Use face and eye primer mandatory |
| Wrong Lighting Application | Different look in party lighting | Test in multiple light conditions |
