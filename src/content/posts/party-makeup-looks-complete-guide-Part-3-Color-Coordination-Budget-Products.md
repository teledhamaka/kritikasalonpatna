## Color Coordination Master Guide {#color-guide}

### **The Color Wheel Principle**

Understanding basic color theory will transform your makeup game:

**Complementary Colors** (Opposites on wheel):
- Red ↔ Green
- Blue ↔ Orange  
- Purple ↔ Yellow

**Analogous Colors** (Next to each other):
- Red, Orange, Yellow
- Blue, Purple, Pink
- Green, Blue, Teal

### **Outfit Color to Makeup Color Chart**

| Outfit Color | Best Eye Colors | Best Lip Colors | Avoid |
|--------------|-----------------|-----------------|-------|
| **Red** | Gold, Bronze, Brown | Nude, Deep Red, Maroon | Pink Lips |
| **Blue** | Silver, Grey, Navy | Pink, Nude, Mauve | Orange Lips |
| **Green** | Gold, Brown, Copper | Nude, Peach, Coral | Red Lips |
| **Pink** | Purple, Silver, Rose | Pink, Nude, Mauve | Orange Lips |
| **Black** | Any Color! | Red, Nude, Berry | Nothing! |
| **White** | Pastels, Neutrals | Bold Red, Pink, Coral | Dark Browns |
| **Gold** | Brown, Bronze, Green | Nude, Peach, Coral | Cool Pinks |
| **Purple** | Silver, Grey, Pink | Mauve, Nude, Berry | Orange Tones |

### **Seasonal Color Guide**

**Summer (Patna Heat):**
- Light colors, minimal makeup
- Waterproof everything
- Pastel shades work well

**Winter:**
- Deeper colors acceptable
- Cream-based products better
- Can go heavier with makeup

**Monsoon:**
- Waterproof mandatory
- Matte finishes only
- Bright colors pop in grey weather

## Shopping Guide Patna {#shopping}

### **Online Shopping:**

**Best Platforms:**
- **Nykaa** - Fastest delivery, authentic products
- **Amazon** - Good deals, wide range
- **MyGlamm** - Indian brand focus

### **Offline Stores in Patna:**

**Premium Stores:**
- **Shoppers Stop** (Fraser Road)
- **Lifestyle** (P&M Mall)
- **MAC Counter** (premium malls)

**Local Beauty Stores:**
- **Boring Road Area** - Multiple beauty stores
- **Patna Junction** - Local makeup shops

### **Shopping Tips for Patna:**

1. **Test Before You Buy:** Always swatch foundations in natural light
2. **Check Expiry Dates:** Especially in local stores
3. **Seasonal Sales:** January and July have best discounts
4. **Local Brands:** Lakme, Sugar work well for Indian skin
